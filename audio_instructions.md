# Audio instructions

- Maximum of 12 audio files
  
- Only MP3 files

- **Optional:** Download these [audios](https://github.com/kingston-hackSpace/Capacitive-Touch-Sensors/blob/main/audios.zip) to test your Touch Board

- **Optional:** Download (free) audios from [Freesound](https://www.freesound.org/)

- **IMPORTANT!** Name your files as follows:

  - TRACK000.mp3
  
  - TRACK001.mp3
  
  - TRACK002.mp3
  
  - ...up to TRACK011.mp3
 
- Turn off your bare-conductive board and removed the micro-SD card

- Save the files in the micro SD card (you may need a SD-card to USB adapter to plug it to your computer)

- Insert the card back in the Bare Conductive Touch Board.

- Done!
